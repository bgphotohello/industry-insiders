Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Peak Green Landscaping ("Peak Green," "we," "us," or "our") operates peakgreentx.com. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our website or submitting a quote request, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: when you request a quote or book an appointment, we collect your name, phone number, email address, property address, the services you're interested in, and any project details you share.
  • Communications: records of calls, text messages, and emails between you and our team.
  • Usage information: basic technical data such as browser type, pages visited, and approximate location, collected through cookies and similar technologies to keep the site working and understand how it's used.

How we use your information

  • To respond to quote requests, schedule and confirm on-site walkthroughs, and provide our services
  • To send appointment confirmations, reminders, and service-related updates
  • With your separate consent, to send occasional offers and seasonal service reminders
  • To improve our website and services, and to comply with legal obligations

Text messaging (SMS) terms

When you check the consent box on our quote form, you agree to receive appointment and account text messages from Peak Green Landscaping at the mobile number you provided (for example, walkthrough confirmations and reminders). If you also check the optional marketing box, you agree to receive occasional offers and seasonal service reminders by text.

  • Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to opt out of text messages. Reply HELP for help, or contact us at (972) 357-5781 or info@peakgreentx.com.
  • Consent to marketing text messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.
  • No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except for vendors and service providers acting on our behalf solely to deliver those messages.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers that help us run our business — such as our customer relationship, scheduling, and messaging platform — who may use it only to provide services to us
  • Professional advisors and authorities when required by law or to protect our rights

Data retention & security

We keep your information for as long as needed to provide services, maintain business records, and meet legal requirements. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect it, though no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices

  • Opt out of texts any time by replying STOP; unsubscribe from emails using the link in any marketing email
  • Ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information by contacting info@peakgreentx.com
  • Set your browser to refuse cookies (parts of the site may not work as intended)

Children's privacy

Our website and services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the current version, and material changes will be posted on this page.
